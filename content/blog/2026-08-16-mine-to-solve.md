---
title: "The Problem I Still Think Is Mine to Solve"
date: 2026-08-16
tldr: "Between two startups, the one whose problem I love most is the one getting the least of me right now. Some honesty about why, and about the crossroads I'm actually at."
---

I run two startups right now, and MDFLD is the one getting neglected. Not because I care about it less. If anything it's the opposite, and that's the part that's hard to admit.

I love the problem MDFLD is trying to solve. Trust in what you're actually buying, gear that is what it claims to be, a layer of honesty in a market that runs on people not knowing what they're getting. It's the kind of problem I think I would still be chasing even if I ended up somewhere else entirely, a different job, a different startup that isn't mine. It feels less like a business idea I'm testing and more like something that's actually mine to solve, whether or not MDFLD the company is the vehicle for it.

And it's the one I've been giving the least time to.

Part of that is just bandwidth. Two startups from one seat means something always loses. But part of it is more honest than that: traction is slow right now, funding hasn't landed, and it's discouraging in a way that's easier to route around by working on the thing that's currently moving instead of the thing I actually care about most. I don't love writing that down. It's true anyway.

I want to be clear about what this isn't. I'm not losing patience with the idea, and I'm not panicking about timing. If anything I'm someone who's usually too relaxed about time for my own good. What's actually thinning is the ambient discouragement, the gap between how much I believe in the problem and how little visible progress there's been lately, and I feel that gap more some weeks than others.

Here's a thing I don't say enough: I'm living at my parents' house right now, for free, and that is the entire reason any of this is possible. If I could wave a magic wand and get one thing, it wouldn't be a funding round or a growth hack. It would be being able to afford to work on MDFLD full time, on my own, without needing anything else to pay the bills first. I'm grateful for the roof. I'm also honest that the roof is a runway with an end on it, and I think about that.

Which is where the other half of this gets honest too. I've never actually been in a structured environment to go deep on the specific technical problem underneath MDFLD, fine-grained visual classification, telling real from fake at the level of detail that matters. Everything I know about it I taught myself, out of necessity, building the thing. I want more than that. I want to learn from people who know meaningfully more than I do, in a setting built for exactly that kind of depth.

So right now I have two Notion boards open. One is jobs. One is grad school. I'm applying to both, seriously, at the same time I keep building MDFLD in whatever hours are left. I don't have a clean answer for where this goes. I'm not going to pretend I do.

What I do know is that the problem doesn't go away for me either way. That part I'm sure of. The rest, I'm figuring out in public, starting with this post.
